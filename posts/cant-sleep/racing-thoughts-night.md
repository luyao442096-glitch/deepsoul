## What Causes Racing Thoughts at Night?

Racing thoughts at night happen when your mind becomes overstimulated or overloaded, making it hard to slow down and rest.

It’s not just overthinking.  
It’s not just stress.

It’s your brain trying to process everything you didn’t have time to deal with during the day.

As explained by [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com), unresolved stress and emotional buildup can lead to increased mental activity, especially when external distractions fade away.

In simple terms, racing thoughts at night mean your mind is still “working” when your body is trying to rest.

In other words, understanding your restless mind means recognizing that it’s trying to process everything you didn’t have space for during the day.

---

## Why This Happens (Science Explained)

Your brain doesn’t shut off instantly.  
It transitions.

- **Cortisol** can stay elevated from stress  
- **Dopamine** fluctuations keep your brain active  
- Your mind builds up **cognitive load** and **emotional load**

So when everything gets quiet, your brain finally has space to process:

> “We didn’t deal with this yet.”

That’s why thoughts speed up at night.

At its core, racing thoughts at night happen because your brain is finally catching up on everything it couldn’t process earlier.
