## What Does Limerence Actually Mean?

Limerence is an intense emotional fixation on someone, often mixed with obsession, dependency, and intrusive thoughts.

It feels like love.  
But it’s unstable.

As explained by [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com), limerence is driven by emotional dependency and uncertainty rather than secure attachment.

In simple terms, limerence means your emotional state depends on how someone else responds to you.

Calming or breaking limerence starts with recognizing whether your feelings are based on emotional stability or emotional dependency.

---

## Why This Happens (Science Explained)

Your brain gets pulled into a loop.

- **Dopamine** spikes when you receive attention  
- **Cortisol** rises when that attention disappears  
- Your mind builds **cognitive load** and **emotional load**

So your brain starts chasing the feeling:

Attention → High → Loss → Anxiety → Craving

At its core, limerence happens because your brain confuses emotional intensity with emotional safety.
