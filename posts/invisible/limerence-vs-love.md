## What Does Limerence vs Love Actually Mean?

Limerence and love can feel similar at the beginning—but psychologically, they are very different.

Limerence is driven by emotional dependency and uncertainty.  
Love is built on stability and mutual connection.

According to [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com), limerence is linked to intrusive thinking and emotional reliance on another person’s validation.

In simple terms:

- Limerence = emotional obsession  
- Love = emotional security  

But here’s where it gets confusing…

Limerence often feels *more intense* than love—especially at the start.

---

## Why This Happens (Psychology Explained)

Your brain reacts differently in limerence vs love.

- **Dopamine** spikes when attention feels unpredictable  
- **Cortisol** rises when there’s uncertainty or distance  
- Your brain builds heavy **cognitive load** and **emotional load**

So your experience becomes:

Uncertainty → Craving → Relief → Anxiety → Repeat

But here’s what most people get wrong:

They think intensity means love.

But psychologically, it’s often the opposite.

The more unstable it feels, the more your brain thinks it matters.

That’s why limerence can feel stronger than love—but be far less real.
